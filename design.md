# Design — OxideTerm

A locked design system for the OxideTerm marketing and documentation site.

## Genre

Cool precision light — clean neutral white and slate surfaces inspired by modern developer-tool interfaces, paired with deep obsidian stages for real product media and terminal output.

## Theme

- `--color-paper`: `#ffffff`
- `--color-paper-2`: `#f7f8fa`
- `--color-paper-3`: `#eef1f5`
- `--color-ink`: `#0d1117`
- `--color-ink-2`: `#374151`
- `--color-muted`: `#5b6472`
- `--color-rule`: `#e2e8f0`
- `--color-accent`: `#d94e28` (oxide copper signal)
- `--color-stage`: `#0b0e14` (cool obsidian stage for media, workbench tabs, and CLI)

## Typography

- Display & UI Body: `Inter` (weights 400–700, tight `-0.03em` tracking on headings)
- Monospace: `JetBrains Mono` (weights 400–500)
- CJK fallback: `PingFang SC`, `Hiragino Sans GB`, `Noto Sans SC`, `Microsoft YaHei`
